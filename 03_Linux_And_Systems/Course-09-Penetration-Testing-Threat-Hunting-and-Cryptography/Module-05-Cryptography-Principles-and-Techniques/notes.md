# Notes

## Importance of Cryptography

- Protects sensitive information from unauthorized access.
- Ensures confidentiality, integrity, and authentication.
- Supports secure communication over untrusted networks.
- Forms the foundation of modern cybersecurity.

## Evolution of Cryptography

- Began with simple substitution and transposition ciphers.
- Advanced into mathematical encryption algorithms.
- Modern cryptography supports digital communication and online security.
- Continues evolving to address emerging cyber threats.

## Encryption, Decryption, and Hashing

### Encryption

- Converts plaintext into ciphertext.
- Protects data confidentiality.
- Requires an encryption key.

### Decryption

- Converts ciphertext back into plaintext.
- Requires the correct decryption key.
- Allows authorized users to access protected information.

### Hashing

- Produces a fixed-length hash value.
- Verifies data integrity.
- One-way process that cannot be reversed.
- Commonly used for password storage and file verification.

## Symmetric Encryption

- Uses a single shared key.
- Fast and efficient.
- Suitable for encrypting large amounts of data.
- Example: AES (Advanced Encryption Standard)

## Asymmetric Encryption

- Uses a public key and a private key.
- Supports secure communication and authentication.
- Enables digital signatures and secure key exchange.
- Example: RSA

## AES (Advanced Encryption Standard)

- Symmetric encryption algorithm.
- Fast and widely adopted.
- Protects files, databases, and network communications.
- Used in many enterprise security solutions.

## RSA Algorithm

- Asymmetric encryption algorithm.
- Uses public and private key pairs.
- Supports secure key exchange.
- Commonly used in SSL/TLS and digital certificates.

## Cryptographic Attacks

- Brute-force attacks
- Dictionary attacks
- Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) attacks
- Replay attacks
- Side-channel attacks
- Weak key attacks

## Cryptanalysis

- Studies methods for breaking cryptographic systems.
- Identifies weaknesses in encryption algorithms.
- Helps improve cryptographic security.
- Evaluates the strength of encryption implementations.

## SSL/TLS and Key Management

- Protects communication between clients and servers.
- Encrypts transmitted data.
- Uses digital certificates for authentication.
- Secure key management is essential for maintaining encryption security.

- Supports future-proof cryptographic security.
- Research continues to develop quantum-resistant algorithms.
