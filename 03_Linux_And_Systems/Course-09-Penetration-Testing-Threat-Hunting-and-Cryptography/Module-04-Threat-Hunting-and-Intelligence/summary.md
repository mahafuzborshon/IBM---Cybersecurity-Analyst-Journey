# Module 04: Threat Hunting and Intelligence

In this module, I learned how organizations proactively search for cyber threats instead of waiting for security alerts. I understood that threat hunting is a proactive security practice where analysts investigate networks and systems to detect hidden threats that may bypass traditional security controls.

I explored the fundamentals of threat intelligence and learned how security teams collect, analyze, and share information about cyber threats. I also learned about the threat intelligence lifecycle and how it transforms raw data into actionable intelligence that helps organizations improve their security posture.

Another important topic was threat intelligence sources. I discovered that intelligence can come from internal logs, open-source intelligence (OSINT), commercial intelligence feeds, and industry reports. I also reviewed IBM X-Force Exchange threat reports to understand how real-world threat intelligence is shared and utilized.

This module also introduced me to Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) systems. I learned how SIEM platforms collect, correlate, and analyze logs from multiple devices to help security analysts detect suspicious activities and respond to incidents more efficiently.

I also explored the growing role of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in threat hunting and threat intelligence. AI helps automate data analysis, detect anomalies, prioritize threats, and reduce the workload of security teams by identifying patterns that might otherwise go unnoticed.

Finally, I learned about User and Entity Behavior Analytics (UEBA), the MITRE ATT&CK Framework, and the Diamond Model of Intrusion Analysis. These frameworks provide structured approaches for understanding attacker behaviors, mapping attack techniques, and improving threat detection strategies.

Overall, this module gave me a solid understanding of proactive cybersecurity practices and how threat intelligence, SIEM platforms, AI, and industry frameworks work together to help organizations detect and respond to advanced cyber threats.
