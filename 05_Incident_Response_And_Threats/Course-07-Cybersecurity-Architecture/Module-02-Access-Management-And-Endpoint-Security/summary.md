# Access Management and Endpoint Security

In this module, I learned how identity and access management helps organizations control and secure user access to systems and resources. I explored how authentication and authorization work together to protect sensitive data and services.

I studied multi-factor authentication (MFA) and understood how adding additional verification methods improves account security and reduces unauthorized access risks. I also practiced applying MFA concepts in different scenarios.

Another important topic in this module was endpoint security. I learned why endpoints are considered one of the biggest attack surfaces in modern environments and how protecting devices is critical for organizational security. The module also introduced different strategies for securing endpoints against threats and unauthorized access.

Overall, this module helped me better understand access control, identity verification, and the importance of securing endpoint devices in cybersecurity architecture.
