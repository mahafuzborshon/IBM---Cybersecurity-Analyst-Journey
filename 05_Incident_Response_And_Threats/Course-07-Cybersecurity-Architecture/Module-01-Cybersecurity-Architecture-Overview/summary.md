# Cybersecurity Architecture Overview

In this module, I explored the foundation of cybersecurity architecture and learned how security principles are applied in real environments. I started with the course overview, expectations, and important preparation guidelines before moving into core security concepts.

I learned about the five major cybersecurity principles and why least privilege is important for reducing unnecessary access. I also studied the CIA Triad and understood how confidentiality, integrity, and availability work together to protect systems and data.

The module also introduced different cybersecurity roles, common security tools, and the OWASP Top 10 vulnerabilities. I explored the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and understood how organizations use structured approaches to improve security operations and risk management.

Overall, this module helped me build a stronger understanding of cybersecurity architecture fundamentals and how security practices are applied across systems and organizations.
