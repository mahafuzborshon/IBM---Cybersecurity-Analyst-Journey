# Notes

## Point of Sale (PoS) Breaches

- Target payment processing systems.
- Steal customers' payment card information.
- Often involve malware or card skimming techniques.
- Can result in financial loss and reputational damage.
- Require strong payment system security controls.

## PoS Malware

- Infects Point of Sale terminals.
- Captures payment card data from memory.
- Sends stolen information to attackers.
- Often remains hidden for long periods.
- Commonly used in retail data breaches.

## Card Skimming

- Steals card information during transactions.
- Can involve physical skimming devices or malware.
- Targets payment terminals and ATMs.
- Used to collect credit and debit card details.

## Target PoS Breach Case Study

- Attackers compromised Target's network.
- PoS malware captured customer payment information.
- Millions of payment cards were affected.
- Demonstrated weaknesses in third-party access and network security.
- Analyzed using the Cyber Kill Chain framework.

## Cyber Kill Chain

- Reconnaissance
- Weaponization
- Delivery
- Exploitation
- Installation
- Command and Control (C2)
- Actions on Objectives

## Home Depot PoS Case Study

- Attackers deployed malware to PoS systems.
- Customer payment data was compromised.
- Highlighted the importance of network segmentation.
- Reinforced the need for continuous monitoring and endpoint security.

## Insider Breaches

- Caused by individuals with authorized access.
- May be intentional or accidental.
- Include employees, contractors, and business partners.
- Can expose sensitive organizational information.
- Often difficult to detect without monitoring.

## Insider Threat Detection

- Monitor privileged accounts.
- Analyze user behavior.
- Implement least privilege access.
- Review access logs regularly.
- Use User and Entity Behavior Analytics (UEBA).
- Conduct security awareness training.

## Vault 7 Case Study

- Demonstrated risks associated with insider data leaks.
- Highlighted the importance of protecting classified information.
- Showed the impact of inadequate internal security controls.

## Pegasus Airlines Case Study

- Sensitive organizational data was exposed.
- Emphasized the importance of secure cloud storage.
- Demonstrated how misconfigurations and insider-related issues can lead to data exposure.

## Mitigating Insider Threats

- Apply the Principle of Least Privilege.
- Implement Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Monitor user activity continuously.
- Encrypt sensitive information.
- Perform regular security audits.
- Enforce strong access control policies.

## Key Takeaways

- PoS systems are common targets for financial cybercrime.
- Malware and skimming techniques can compromise payment data.
- The Cyber Kill Chain helps analyze attack progression.
- Insider threats remain one of the most challenging security risks.
- Continuous monitoring, strong access controls, and user behavior analysis help reduce insider threats.
- Real-world case studies provide valuable lessons for improving organizational cybersecurity.
