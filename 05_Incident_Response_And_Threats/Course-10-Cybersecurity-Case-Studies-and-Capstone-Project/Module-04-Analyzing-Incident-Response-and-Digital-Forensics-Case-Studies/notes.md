# Notes

## Incident Response

- Structured process for managing cybersecurity incidents.
- Helps minimize damage and restore normal operations.
- Supports business continuity and security improvements.
- Requires preparation, coordination, and documentation.

## Incident Response Lifecycle

- Preparation
- Identification
- Containment
- Eradication
- Recovery
- Lessons Learned

## Google Home Case Study

- Demonstrated a real-world incident response scenario.
- Showed the importance of identifying security issues quickly.
- Highlighted communication and coordinated response efforts.
- Reinforced the value of continuous security monitoring.

## Lightning Strikes Case Study

- Explored how organizations respond to unexpected incidents.
- Emphasized business continuity and disaster recovery planning.
- Demonstrated the importance of timely decision-making.
- Highlighted lessons learned from incident response failures.

## When Incident Response Goes Wrong

- Delayed detection increases damage.
- Poor communication slows recovery.
- Lack of preparation creates confusion.
- Incomplete documentation affects future investigations.
- Weak security controls increase organizational risk.

## Digital Forensics

- Collects and analyzes digital evidence.
- Supports cybersecurity investigations.
- Helps determine how incidents occurred.
- Preserves evidence for legal proceedings.
- Assists law enforcement and incident response teams.

## Digital Forensics Process

- Identification
- Preservation
- Collection
- Examination
- Analysis
- Reporting
- Presentation

## Chain of Custody

- Documents how evidence is handled.
- Maintains evidence integrity.
- Records every transfer of evidence.
- Ensures legal admissibility.

## Bernard Madoff Case Study

- Demonstrated the importance of digital evidence during financial crime investigations.
- Showed how forensic analysis supports legal proceedings.
- Highlighted the role of documentation and evidence preservation.

## United States v. Ganias Case Study

- Focused on digital evidence handling and privacy concerns.
- Demonstrated legal challenges involving forensic investigations.
- Reinforced the importance of following proper legal procedures.

## Responding to a Digital Forensics Warrant

- Verify the warrant.
- Preserve relevant digital evidence.
- Maintain the chain of custody.
- Cooperate with authorized investigators.
- Document every action taken.
