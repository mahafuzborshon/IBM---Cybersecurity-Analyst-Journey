# Module 04: Analyzing Incident Response and Digital Forensics Case Studies

In this module, I learned how organizations respond to cybersecurity incidents and investigate digital evidence after an attack. Through several real-world case studies, I gained a better understanding of how incident response and digital forensics work together to minimize damage, recover systems, and support legal investigations.

I first explored the incident response process and learned the importance of preparing for security incidents before they occur. I studied how organizations identify, contain, eradicate, and recover from cyberattacks while documenting every step to improve future security practices.

The Google Home and Lightning Strikes case studies helped me understand how different incidents require different response strategies. I also learned that poor planning or delayed responses can increase the impact of an attack, making preparation and communication critical during an incident.

The second half of the module introduced me to digital forensics. I learned how investigators collect, preserve, examine, and analyze digital evidence without altering its integrity. Maintaining a proper chain of custody and following legal procedures are essential to ensure that evidence remains admissible during investigations.

Through the Bernard Madoff and United States versus Ganias case studies, I explored how digital evidence can support criminal investigations and legal proceedings. I also completed an activity on responding to a digital forensics warrant, which helped me understand the legal responsibilities organizations face when handling digital evidence.

Overall, this module strengthened my understanding of incident response methodologies, digital forensic investigations, evidence handling, and the importance of following structured procedures during cybersecurity incidents.
