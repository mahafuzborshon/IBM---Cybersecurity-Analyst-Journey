# Module 05: Analyzing Penetration Testing and Compliance Case Studies

In this module, I explored how penetration testing and cybersecurity compliance help organizations identify security weaknesses and meet regulatory requirements. Through several real-world case studies, I learned how security assessments and compliance programs work together to reduce cyber risks.

I first reviewed the fundamentals of penetration testing and analyzed the Equifax data breach. This case study showed how an unpatched vulnerability and delayed remediation allowed attackers to compromise sensitive customer information. It reinforced the importance of vulnerability management, timely patching, and continuous security testing.

I also explored the IBM X-Force Red case study, where I learned how professional penetration testing teams simulate real-world attacks to evaluate an organization's security posture. This helped me understand how penetration testing identifies weaknesses before attackers can exploit them.

The second half of the module focused on cybersecurity compliance. I learned why organizations must follow security regulations, industry standards, and legal requirements to protect sensitive information and reduce organizational risk.

The Marriott and Equiniti compliance case studies demonstrated how failures in security governance and regulatory compliance can lead to major data breaches, financial penalties, and reputational damage. I also learned about some of the largest cybersecurity fines in history, highlighting the consequences of failing to protect customer data.

Overall, this module helped me understand how penetration testing and compliance complement each other by identifying vulnerabilities, improving security controls, and ensuring organizations meet industry and regulatory expectations.
