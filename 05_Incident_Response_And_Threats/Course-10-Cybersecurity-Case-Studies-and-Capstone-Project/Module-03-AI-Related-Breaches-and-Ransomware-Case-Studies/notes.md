# Notes

## AI-Related Breaches

- Involve the misuse of Artificial Intelligence technologies.
- Can enhance social engineering and fraud attacks.
- Often use deepfake audio, video, or generated content.
- Target individuals and organizations through deception.
- Continue to evolve as AI technology advances.

## Deepfake Attacks

- Use AI to imitate voices, faces, or videos.
- Can deceive employees into performing unauthorized actions.
- Increase the effectiveness of phishing and Business Email Compromise (BEC) attacks.
- Require stronger identity verification procedures.

## Deepfake Conference Call Case Study

- Demonstrated how AI-generated voices can impersonate trusted executives.
- Social engineering led to unauthorized financial transactions.
- Highlighted the importance of verification before acting on sensitive requests.
- Reinforced the need for employee security awareness.

## Notable AI-Related Breaches

- Show how AI can support sophisticated cyberattacks.
- Demonstrate risks associated with identity impersonation.
- Emphasize the importance of AI security governance.
- Encourage organizations to strengthen authentication and monitoring.

## Ransomware Breaches

- Malware encrypts files and systems.
- Attackers demand ransom payments for decryption.
- Can disrupt business operations and critical services.
- Often spreads through phishing emails, vulnerabilities, or compromised credentials.

## Emerging Ransomware Trends

- Double extortion attacks.
- Ransomware-as-a-Service (RaaS).
- Targeting critical infrastructure.
- Data theft before encryption.
- Increased use of phishing and remote access attacks.

## City of Atlanta Case Study

- Ransomware disrupted city government services.
- Impacted multiple departments and public operations.
- Required extensive recovery efforts.
- Demonstrated the high cost of ransomware incidents.

## Colonial Pipeline Case Study

- Ransomware affected critical infrastructure.
- Caused fuel distribution disruptions.
- Highlighted the importance of securing operational technology (OT) environments.
- Demonstrated the national impact of cyberattacks.

## Responding to Ransomware

- Isolate infected systems immediately.
- Report the incident.
- Restore data from secure backups.
- Patch known vulnerabilities.
- Strengthen endpoint protection.
- Conduct post-incident analysis.

## Best Practices

- Maintain offline and tested backups.
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Keep software updated.
- Train employees to recognize phishing attempts.
- Monitor networks continuously.
- Develop and test an incident response plan.

- AI can be misused to strengthen social engineering attacks.
- Deepfake technology creates new identity verification challenges.
- Ransomware remains one of the most destructive cyber threats.
