# Module 03: AI-Related Breaches and Ransomware Case Studies

In this module, I explored how emerging technologies like Artificial Intelligence (AI) are influencing modern cybersecurity threats. Through several real-world case studies, I learned how attackers misuse AI technologies and how ransomware continues to be one of the most damaging cyber threats affecting organizations worldwide.

I first learned about AI-related breaches and how deepfake technology can be used for fraud and social engineering attacks. The Deepfake Conference Call case study showed how attackers can imitate a person's voice or appearance to deceive employees into transferring money or revealing sensitive information. This demonstrated the growing risks associated with AI-powered cyberattacks.

I also explored several notable AI-related security incidents and learned how organizations must strengthen identity verification, employee awareness, and security controls to defend against these evolving threats.

The second half of the module focused on ransomware attacks. I learned how ransomware encrypts victims' files, disrupts business operations, and demands payment for data recovery. I also reviewed emerging ransomware trends and understood why ransomware remains one of the most significant cybersecurity threats today.

Through the City of Atlanta and Colonial Pipeline case studies, I analyzed how ransomware attacks can severely impact public services, critical infrastructure, and business continuity. These real-world examples highlighted the importance of incident response planning, regular backups, patch management, and proactive cybersecurity measures.

Overall, this module helped me understand how AI-related attacks and ransomware continue to evolve, emphasizing the need for stronger security awareness, modern defensive strategies, and effective incident response planning.
