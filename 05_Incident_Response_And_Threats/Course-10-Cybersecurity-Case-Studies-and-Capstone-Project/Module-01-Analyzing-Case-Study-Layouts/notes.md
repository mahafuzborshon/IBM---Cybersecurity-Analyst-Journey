# Notes

## Cybersecurity Case Studies

- Analyze real-world cybersecurity incidents.
- Help understand attack methods and security failures.
- Demonstrate how organizations respond to cyber threats.
- Provide practical lessons for improving security.
- Support critical thinking and incident analysis.

## Analyzing Case Studies

An effective case study should examine:

- Background of the incident
- Threat actors
- Attack methods
- Vulnerabilities exploited
- Business impact
- Incident response
- Lessons learned
- Security recommendations

## Effective Case Study Layout

- Introduction
- Organization background
- Timeline of events
- Attack overview
- Root cause analysis
- Response and recovery
- Recommendations
- Key takeaways

## Phishing Scams

- Social engineering attack designed to steal sensitive information.
- Often delivered through emails, messages, or fake websites.
- Tricks victims into revealing credentials or financial information.
- Remains one of the most common cyber threats.

## Common Phishing Indicators

- Suspicious sender addresses
- Urgent or threatening language
- Unexpected attachments
- Fake login pages
- Misspelled URLs
- Requests for confidential information

## Cisco Cyber Threat Trends Report

- Highlights current cybersecurity threats.
- Shows phishing remains a major attack vector.
- Provides insights into emerging attack techniques.
- Helps organizations improve security awareness.

## Google and Facebook Phishing Case Study

- Attackers impersonated legitimate vendors.
- Used fraudulent invoices and emails.
- Social engineering led to significant financial losses.
- Demonstrated the importance of verification procedures.

## South Korean Doctor Vishing Case Study

- Example of voice phishing (vishing).
- Attackers manipulated victims over phone calls.
- Used trust and urgency to steal information.
- Showed the risks of social engineering attacks.

## Responding to Phishing Attacks

- Verify suspicious emails or calls.
- Do not click unknown links.
- Report phishing attempts immediately.
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Keep security awareness training up to date.
- Monitor accounts for unusual activity.
