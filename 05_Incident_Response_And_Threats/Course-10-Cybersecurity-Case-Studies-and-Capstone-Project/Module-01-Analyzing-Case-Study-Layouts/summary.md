# Module 01: Analyzing Case Study Layouts

In this module, I learned how cybersecurity case studies help security professionals understand real-world incidents and improve decision-making. I explored the structure of a cybersecurity case study and learned how to analyze each section to identify key events, security failures, and lessons learned.

I learned an effective approach for analyzing case studies by focusing on the background of the incident, identifying the attack methods, understanding the impact, evaluating the response, and reviewing the recommendations. This structured approach makes it easier to understand how security incidents unfold and how organizations can prevent similar attacks.

A major part of this module focused on phishing scams. I learned how phishing attacks use social engineering techniques to trick individuals into revealing sensitive information or performing harmful actions. I also reviewed current cyber threat trends to better understand why phishing remains one of the most common attack methods.

Through the Google and Facebook phishing case study, I learned how attackers successfully impersonated trusted individuals to steal millions of dollars from large organizations. I also explored the South Korean Doctor vishing case study, which demonstrated how voice phishing attacks manipulate victims into sharing confidential information or transferring money.

Overall, this module strengthened my ability to analyze cybersecurity incidents, recognize phishing techniques, and apply lessons learned from real-world case studies to improve cybersecurity awareness and incident response.
