# Notes

## Information Technology Infrastructure Library (ITIL)
- ITIL is a framework for managing and improving IT services.
- Focuses on delivering value to customers through effective service management.
- Supports consistent service delivery and continual improvement.
- Helps align IT services with business objectives.
- Encourages standardized processes and best practices.

## Risk Management
- Risk management helps organizations identify potential threats and vulnerabilities.
- Risks are analyzed based on likelihood and impact.
- Risk treatment options include mitigation, transfer, acceptance, and avoidance.
- Continuous monitoring is important for managing changing risks.
- Risk-based decision-making supports organizational resilience.

## AI Ethics and Governance
- AI systems should be used responsibly and ethically.
- Transparency helps users understand AI-driven decisions.
- Accountability ensures responsibility for AI outcomes.
- Fairness reduces bias and discriminatory impacts.
- Governance frameworks guide the safe and compliant use of AI technologies.

## Key Takeaways
- ITSM improves the quality and reliability of IT services.
- ITIL provides a structured approach to service management.
- Risk management supports informed business decisions.
- AI governance promotes responsible and trustworthy AI usage.
- Effective governance helps organizations balance innovation, security, and compliance.
