# Module 02: Foundations of IT Service Management and Risk Governance

In this module, I learned the fundamentals of IT Service Management (ITSM) and how organizations use structured processes to deliver and support IT services effectively. I explored the IT Infrastructure Library (ITIL) framework and its role in improving service quality and operational efficiency.

I also studied risk management concepts, including how organizations identify, assess, and respond to risks that could impact business operations. Another important topic was AI ethics and governance, where I learned about responsible AI use, accountability, transparency, and ethical decision-making.

This module helped me understand how service management, risk governance, and emerging technologies work together to support secure and reliable business operations.
