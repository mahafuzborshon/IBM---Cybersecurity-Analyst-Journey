# Module 01: Introduction to Information Security and Compliance

In this module, I learned why information security and compliance are important for protecting organizational data and maintaining trust. I explored the relationship between security policies, regulations, and business objectives.

I gained an understanding of Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) and how organizations use these practices to manage security responsibilities and reduce risk. I also learned about common cybersecurity frameworks and how they help organizations build structured security programs.

Another key area was security management and oversight, including the roles of leadership, policies, standards, and accountability in maintaining a strong security posture. This module provided a solid foundation for understanding how compliance requirements and security practices work together to protect information assets.
