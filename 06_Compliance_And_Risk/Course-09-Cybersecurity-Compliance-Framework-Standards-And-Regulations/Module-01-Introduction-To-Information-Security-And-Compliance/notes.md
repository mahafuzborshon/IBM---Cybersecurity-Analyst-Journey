## Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)
- Governance establishes security direction and accountability.
- Risk management identifies, analyzes, and mitigates threats.
- Compliance ensures adherence to laws, regulations, and policies.
- GRC helps organizations align security with business goals.
- Risk assessments support informed security decisions.

## Cybersecurity Framework
- Frameworks provide structured guidance for security programs.
- Help organizations manage and improve cybersecurity practices.
- Support risk-based security planning.
- Promote consistency across security operations.
- Frameworks assist with compliance and audit preparation.

## Security Management and Oversight
- Leadership plays a critical role in security governance.
- Security policies define organizational expectations.
- Standards and procedures support policy implementation.
- Oversight helps monitor security effectiveness.
- Continuous review and improvement strengthen security posture.

## Key Takeaways
- Information security and compliance are closely connected.
- GRC provides a foundation for managing organizational security.
- Cybersecurity frameworks offer structured security guidance.
- Effective oversight ensures accountability and continuous improvement.
- Security programs must align with organizational objectives and regulatory requirements.
