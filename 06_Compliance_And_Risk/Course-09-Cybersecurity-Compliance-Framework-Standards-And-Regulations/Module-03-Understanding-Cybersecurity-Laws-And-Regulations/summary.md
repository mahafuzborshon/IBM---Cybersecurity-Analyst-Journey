# Module 03: Understanding Cybersecurity Laws and Regulations

In this module, I learned about the legal and regulatory requirements that organizations must follow to protect information and maintain compliance. I explored major cybersecurity laws and regulations from both US and global perspectives and gained a better understanding of how these requirements influence security practices.

I studied important regulations such as HIPAA, GDPR, and PCI DSS, along with their objectives and compliance expectations. The module also highlighted the importance of protecting sensitive data, respecting privacy rights, and maintaining regulatory compliance across different regions.

This module helped me understand how cybersecurity, privacy, and legal obligations work together to support secure and responsible business operations.
