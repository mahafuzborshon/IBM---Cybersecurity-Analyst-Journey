# Module 04: Understanding Cybersecurity Standards and Audits

In this module, I learned about the role of cybersecurity standards and audits in maintaining secure and compliant environments. I explored several widely recognized industry standards and how organizations use them to establish security best practices.

I studied OWASP and its practical use cases for improving application security. I also learned about NIST, ISO, and IEEE standards and how they provide guidance for managing cybersecurity programs, processes, and technologies.

Another important topic was security audits. I learned how audits help evaluate compliance, identify gaps, verify security controls, and support continuous improvement. This module strengthened my understanding of how standards and audits work together to improve an organization's overall security posture.
